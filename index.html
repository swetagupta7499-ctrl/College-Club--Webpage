--- WHO WE ARE ---

About College Club Hub

Empowering students beyond classrooms through innovation, culture, and teamwork.

🌟 Introduction

College Club Hub is our college's official student-driven platform where students across all batches come together to share ideas, learn new skills, and build leadership qualities.

🎯 Our Mission

Our campus features 7 vibrant student clubs: Coding Club, AI Club, Music Club, Drama Club, Sports Club, Debate Club, and Art & Craft Club. We unite all these communities under one roof so every student can easily discover their passion and participate in workshops, hackathons, cultural fests, and sports tournaments.
